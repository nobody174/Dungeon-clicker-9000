Privacy Policy — Dungeon Clicker 9000

Last updated: 2026-08-01

Dungeon Clicker 9000 does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

What the game stores

Your game progress (gold, floor, equipment, etc.) is saved only on your own device, using your browser's or app's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or to any third party. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data deletes it permanently.

No accounts, no tracking

The game does not require sign-in, does not use analytics, does not include advertising, and makes no network requests to any server other than the one hosting the game's own files.
