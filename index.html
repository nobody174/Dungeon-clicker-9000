How Boss Dodge works

On a boss floor, a player HP indicator and Dodge button appear. Every few seconds the boss "winds up" (a visible warning cue) before attacking. Press Dodge (or the D key) during that warning window to avoid the hit entirely. Missing the window costs 1 HP and a small gold penalty — it does not end your run. If you're idle/not actively playing, hits auto-dodge so offline progress is never punished.
